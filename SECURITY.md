# Security Policy

## Supported versions

ToolCommons is currently pre-alpha. Security fixes are applied to the latest
revision of the `main` branch; older commits and generated benchmark receipts are
not maintained release lines.

Receipts demonstrate reproducibility under declared conditions. They are not
security attestations, endorsements, or proof that an integrated tool is safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability that could put users,
systems, credentials, or unpublished data at risk. Report it privately to
[linhaquenaoquebra@gmail.com](mailto:linhaquenaoquebra@gmail.com) with:

- the affected component and revision;
- the impact you believe is possible;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- any known mitigations;
- whether and when you intend to disclose the issue publicly.

Do not include real secrets, personal data, or destructive payloads. Use synthetic
examples wherever possible.

We aim to acknowledge a report within seven days. We will then assess severity,
agree on a disclosure path where practical, and publish a fix or mitigation as
soon as responsibly possible. This is a volunteer project, so timing may vary;
silence beyond seven days is a reason to send a follow-up.

Good-faith research that respects privacy, avoids service disruption, and allows
reasonable time for remediation is welcome. This policy does not authorise access
to systems or data without permission.
