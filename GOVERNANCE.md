# ToolCommons Governance

ToolCommons is maintained as a public evidence commons. Governance should remain
lightweight while the project is pre-alpha and become more distributed as regular
contributors emerge.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose capabilities, tasks, adapters, transformations,
  reproductions, documentation, and fixes.
- **Reviewers** provide evidence-based feedback and may be invited to review areas
  where they have demonstrated experience.
- **Maintainers** merge changes, manage releases and repository settings, respond
  to security and conduct reports, and preserve the integrity of the evidence
  model.

## Decisions

Most decisions are made through public issues and pull requests. The default path
is rough consensus supported by reproducible evidence. Maintainers make the final
merge decision when consensus is unclear, explaining material trade-offs in
public unless privacy or security prevents it.

Changes to schemas, evidence semantics, licensing, governance, or compatibility
should begin with an issue and allow reasonable time for review. Urgent security
fixes may be merged first and explained after disclosure is safe.

No tool receives preferential treatment because of popularity, sponsorship, or a
maintainer's affiliation. Relevant affiliations must be disclosed. Conflicting or
negative receipts remain valid contributions when they meet the same provenance
and reproducibility standards.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers may invite contributors who have made sustained, constructive
contributions, reviewed others fairly, and shown care for provenance and community
safety. Maintainer access may be removed after prolonged inactivity, misuse of
access, or serious violations, following a documented review.

As the maintainer group grows, this document should be revised to define voting,
quorum, succession, and an appeal path without concentrating decisions in a single
person or organisation.
