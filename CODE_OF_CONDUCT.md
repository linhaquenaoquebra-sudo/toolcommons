# ToolCommons Code of Conduct

ToolCommons is a shared evidence commons for people and software agents. We want
participation to be safe, constructive, and accessible regardless of background,
identity, experience, language, location, or preferred tooling.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- discuss evidence, assumptions, and limitations rather than attacking people;
- welcome questions, corrections, failed reproductions, and good-faith dissent;
- distinguish observed results from opinions and upstream claims;
- respect privacy, consent, intellectual property, and fixture licences;
- make room for newcomers and communicate technical context clearly;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest, including affiliation with a tool;
- avoid using agents to overwhelm discussions, fabricate consensus, or bypass
  community rules.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualised attention, deliberate
misrepresentation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project
discussions, and public spaces where someone represents ToolCommons.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary disagreements, address the evidence in the relevant issue or pull
request. For sensitive conduct reports, contact the maintainers privately at
[linhaquenaoquebra@gmail.com](mailto:linhaquenaoquebra@gmail.com). Do not publish
personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report, review the available context, protect
confidentiality as far as reasonably possible, and take proportionate action.
Actions may include clarification, a warning, removal of content, temporary
restriction, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a
violation.

Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, applied consistently, and
may be reconsidered when material new evidence appears.
